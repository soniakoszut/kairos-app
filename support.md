# Kairos Support

**Contact:** soniakoszut+kairos@gmail.com

**Is my data private?** Yes. It stays on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is sent to me.

**Why are my forecasts all the same?** Kairos needs real data. Check in daily (the evening check-in matters most), log your period if you track a cycle, and connect Health and Calendar. Forecasts become personal after about 10 days.

**Can Kairos read my Flo data?** Flo doesn't share period data with Apple Health. Add your past periods in Profile › Cycle › Add Past Periods.

**How do I move to a new iPhone?** In Profile › Data Sources, choose an iCloud Drive folder for backups (or export a backup). On the new iPhone, install Kairos and use Profile › Data Sources › Restore from Backup.

**How do I start setup again?** Profile › Restart Onboarding.

**How do I delete my data?** Profile › Data Sources › Delete All Data. Backups you saved to your own iCloud Drive folder can be deleted in the Files app.
