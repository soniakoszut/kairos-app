# Kairos Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Kairos is made by Sonia Koszut ("I"). This policy explains what Kairos does with your information. Short version: **your data stays with you.**

## What Kairos stores
Your check-ins, notes, meals, period days and settings are stored **only on your iPhone**. There is no Kairos server or account, so I can't see or access them.

## What Kairos reads
- **Apple Health** (only what you allow): sleep, steps, active energy, workouts, mindful minutes, menstrual flow, dietary protein and water. Used only on your iPhone to explain and forecast your days.
- **Calendar** (only if you allow): your events, to work out how busy each day is. Kairos adds an event only when you ask it to.
Health and calendar data are never copied off your device by Kairos, never sold, and never used for advertising.

## What is sent over the internet
Only one thing: when you search for a packaged food, the **search text** is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look it up. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

## What Kairos does not do
No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. Optional diagnostic reports are only shared if you choose to send them.

## Backups and deleting your data
Kairos saves backups on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Kairos) and, if you choose, in a folder in your iCloud Drive. You can export everything, or delete all Kairos data, in Profile › Data Sources. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone; your own iCloud Drive backups stay until you delete them.

## Children
Kairos is not intended for children under 13.

## Medical disclaimer
Kairos gives general wellbeing information. Forecasts and suggestions are estimates based on your own data, not medical advice. Kairos is not a medical device, contraceptive or fertility tool, and doesn't diagnose any condition.
